# How to Return to Zwift Racing

Zwift needs recent activity data before it can calculate a Racing Score and admit a rider to scored races.

## Complete a qualifying activity

As of **13 September 2026**, Zwift's Racing Score FAQ says to:

1. Check that your weight is accurate in your Zwift profile.
2. Complete and save at least one Zwift cycling activity **longer than 10 minutes**.
3. Wait for Zwift to process the activity, then check your Racing Score in Zwift or the Zwift Companion app.

A ride that only clears the minimum may generate a score without representing your fitness well. For better initial seeding, Zwift recommends efforts longer than 10 minutes containing a mixture of steady and high-intensity riding. Because the initial seed uses recent five-minute power, a practical return ride is 20–30 minutes with a warm-up and a representative sustained five-minute hard effort. Do not perform a maximal effort unless it is safe and appropriate for you.

Once a score appears, enter the score range Zwift permits. Individual races can impose additional equipment, heart-rate, verification, or organiser-specific rules.

## Why older instructions differ

Zwift introduced a requirement for three activities of at least 10 minutes in September 2024. It announced in November 2024 that the requirement would be reduced to one activity. Old forum posts, articles, and search results may therefore still say that three rides are required.

## Check for revised requirements

Do not treat the numbers in this guide as permanent. Before returning after a long break:

1. Read the official [Zwift Racing Score FAQ](https://support.zwift.com/en_us/racing-score-faq-BkG9_Rqrh). This is the primary source for current eligibility and score behaviour.
2. Read [How Your Zwift Racing Score Is Calculated](https://support.zwift.com/en_us/how-your-zwift-racing-score-is-calculated-Skzjiybyzx) for the current seed and update inputs.
3. Check the official [Zwift Racing forum](https://forums.zwift.com/c/racing/17) for announced changes and current known issues.
4. Check the event description for rules specific to the race you intend to enter.
